Word & Wonder  · /build your own learning app with Claude/

A page that teaches you something new every day: a language in the morning and general knowledge in the evening. Claude builds it, fills it daily, and pings you when it's ready.

How to use it (2 minutes)

1.  Replace everything highlighted in [brackets] with what YOU want. Learning Spanish instead of Danish? Want cooking instead of history? Just change the word.
2.  Copy the box below and paste it into Claude (the app or claude.ai).
3.  Answer Claude's questions and approve the scheduled tasks. Your first lesson arrives the next morning.

Build my learning app
Build me a daily learning app called [App name] as a Claude artifact page, with a small database so new lessons can be added every day.
About me

I want to learn [language] at level [beginner / A2 / B1]. Track 2 (optional): [a second language or skill, or delete this line]

Track 3 · Brain Food every evening
One short general-knowledge lesson I can play like a story: 5 to 7 slides read aloud. Then a chat or debate, a short poem that teaches 3 new [English] words, a quiz and real sources. Topics by day: Mon [business], Tue [marketing], Wed [history], Thu [world news explained], Fri [arts & culture], Sat [science], Sun [the week in news]. Check facts with reliable sources and stay neutral.

The page
Phone-friendly, one tab per track, flip cards, read-aloud buttons, and a deck that saves every word for review.

The schedule
